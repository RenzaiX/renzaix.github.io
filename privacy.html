Privacy Policy

TwitchDownloader · last updated November 15, 2026

TwitchDownloader is a personal desktop tool for Windows. Its owner uses it on their own computer to download stream recordings, edit them into videos, and upload those videos to their own YouTube channels and Google Drive folders. It is not offered to the public and has no server: everything it does runs on the owner's computer.

Google data the app uses

When you sign in with Google, you choose which access to allow. The app may ask for:

  • YouTube (manage your YouTube account): to upload videos and to set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and visibility on your own channels, and to read your own channels' and videos' details.
  • Google Drive: to read videos from your own Drive folders and to upload finished videos into them.

The app uses this access only to do what you ask it to do with your own channels and files.

How the data is kept

  • Sign-in tokens and any data the app reads are stored only on your own computer.
  • The app does not send your Google data to the developer or to any other server, and it does not sell, rent or share it with anyone.
  • Google user data is not used for advertising, and it is not used to train AI or machine-learning models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

TwitchDownloader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's folder on your computer deletes everything it stored.

Contact

Questions: use the support email shown on Google's sign-in screen for this app.
